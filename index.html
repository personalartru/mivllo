Meet the Characters

Mivllo

Music & Rhythm

The fluffiest pink-and-lavender kitten. Loves to sing, and his tummy glows with a magical musical note.

Pip

Nature & Voice

A tiny mint-green bird with soft, dandelion-like feathers. Teaches toddlers nature sounds and new words.

Piko

Bedtime Stories

A peach-colored fluffy hedgehog without spikes. The keeper of cozy bedtime stories with a glowing star on his tummy.

Bubu

Games & Fun

A sky-blue bunny with long, soft ears. Loves hide-and-seek, jumping, and learning colors with little ones.
